# GSV Poems — 3D icon render prompts

These prompts use your template, filled in for each icon in the Mor (peacock) palette. Paste one into your image tool (Midjourney, DALL·E, Imagen, Firefly...) to render a photoreal version.

- **In the app today:** the vector 3D icons from the design canvas (`Icon3D` component). They stay sharp at every size, follow the theme colours and work offline.
- **To use renders instead:** export each one as a 1024×1024 PNG with a transparent or #F4F7F6 background, name it `<key>.png` (for example `home.png`), and put it in `assets/icons/3d/`. I will switch the app to those images and keep the vector icons as a fallback.
- **Night theme:** change "Light theme background" to "Dark theme background" and #F4F7F6 to #0E1416.
